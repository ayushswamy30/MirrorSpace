How it works

It notices, so you don’t have to keep score.

Most days you just name how you are. Over weeks, Lowkei finds what lifts you and what drains you — from your own words, on your own phone.

01

Check in.

Pick heavy, wound up, bright or easy, then the closest word. Add what it was around if you like.

02

Put it down.

Vent is a page nobody else reads. Keep it, or let it go when you’re done.

03

See the weather.

Thirty days become a ring of Inner Weather — clear, mild, overcast, fog or storm.

Inside Lowkei

Small, honest tools for ordinary days.

Today

A reading built from your own patterns.

Each morning, Lowkei turns your check-ins and sleep into a short reading — what tends to help, what to go easy on. It’s worked out on your phone, from your numbers, never your words.

  • Do and don’t, from what’s actually lifted you
  • Share the day’s reading as a card
  • Low-day mode asks for nothing when things are heavy
Your Mind Chart

Your placements, drawn from ninety days.

When you’ve checked in for two weeks, Lowkei draws your Mind Chart: your nights, your lightest hours, how heavy days show up, and what tends to restore or drain you.

  • Every placement shows the numbers behind it
  • Placements still filling in say so
  • Wrapped tells the month back, from day thirty
The Mirror

Ask the question you keep circling.

The Mirror is an AI you can talk things through with — software, not a person, and not a therapist. It only switches on if you say so, and it only knows what you choose to show it.

  • Tap speak to talk instead of type; your phone turns it into text
  • “What the Mirror knows” is a page you can read and edit
  • Answers that rely on it say which note they used
Circle

Let a few people see your weather. Nothing else.

Add the people you trust. They see one word for your day and when you’re running low — never your words, notes or check-ins. A nudge says “thinking of you” without a single question.

  • Twelve people at most, by invite code
  • “Running low” clears itself after a day
  • Alerts never show names or feelings on a lock screen
Calm

Breathe out for longer than you breathe in.

Guided breathing, grounding and sounds, one tap from every screen. If it’s more than a bad day, crisis lines for your country and your own safety plan are right there — free, always.

  • Double sigh, box breathing and 4·7·8
  • A safety plan you write for yourself
  • Crisis lines for your country

Privacy

Private by design, not by setting.
