How it works

It notices, so you don’t have to keep score.

Most days you just name how you are. Over the weeks, Lowkei finds what lifts you and what drains you — from your own check-ins, on your own phone.

  1. 01

    Check in.

    Pick wound up, bright, heavy or easy, then the word that fits. Add what it was around, if you like.

  2. 02

    Put it down.

    Vent is a page nobody else reads. Keep it, or let it go when you’re done.

  3. 03

    See the weather.

    A week of check-ins becomes a ring of Inner Weather — clear, mild, overcast, fog or storm — and the app takes on the colour of your day.

  4. 04

    Read your day.

    Each morning, a short reading built from your own patterns: what tends to help, and what to go easy on.

Inside Lowkei

Small, gentle tools for ordinary days.

Circle

Let a few people see your weather — one word for your day, and when you’re running low. Never your words.

Calm

Breathing, grounding and sounds, one tap from every screen. Crisis lines are right there, always free.

Mind Chart

Your placements, drawn from ninety days: your nights, your lightest hours, what restores you.

Wrapped

The month, or the year, told back to you — plain facts, no scores.

The Mirror

Ask the question you keep circling.

An AI you can talk things through with — software, not a person, and not a therapist. It opens on your third day, only if you switch it on, and only knows what you choose to show it.

  • Tap speak to talk instead of type
  • “What the Mirror knows” is a page you can read and edit
  • Answers that use it say which note they used
Privacy

Private by design, not by setting.

What you write is kept on your phone, not on our servers. You can download all of it, or erase all of it, from the app.
