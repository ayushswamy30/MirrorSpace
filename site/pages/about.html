About

A journal that doesn’t need to know who you are.

Lowkei is made by a small independent team in India. We wanted somewhere to put a hard day that wouldn’t ask for an account, sell an ad, or keep a copy.

What we promise

  • Your words stay on your phone. Check-ins, pages, letters and Mirror chats are encrypted on the device, not uploaded.
  • No ads, no trackers. There is no advertising or analytics code in the app or on this site.
  • AI only when you ask. The Mirror is off until you switch it on, and it says plainly that it’s software, not a person.
  • Help is never paywalled. Crisis lines, calm and your safety plan are free, always.
  • Leaving is easy. Download everything, or erase everything, in one tap.

How Lowkei looks

Lowkei is designed like a printed almanac: paper and ink, rules instead of boxes, and one colour — the Inner Weather — kept to small marks. The illustrations are ink drawings, chosen to feel like something you’d find pressed in a book.

What Lowkei is not

Lowkei isn’t therapy, a diagnosis or an emergency service. It can help you notice patterns and get through a moment, and it will point you to people who can help when that’s what you need.
