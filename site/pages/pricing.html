Plus isn’t on sale yet. It opens with Lowkei’s Play Store release. Until then, everything in the app is free to use.

Billing

Plus is a monthly subscription, bought and managed through the Google Play Store. It renews each month until you cancel, and the Play Store shows the price in your currency before you confirm. Outside India, prices are set in US dollars or the local equivalent.

Cancelling

Cancel any time in the Play Store: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Lowkei. Plus keeps working until the end of the month you’ve paid for. Your check-ins, pages and charts are never deleted because a subscription ends.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. If something went wrong, write to us as well and we’ll help.

Student pricing

The student price is for people currently enrolled at a college or university. We may ask for proof of enrolment, and may move a subscription to the standard price if it can’t be shown.

What stays free

Crisis help, your safety plan, calm, check-ins and vent will never be behind a subscription. Erasing or downloading your data is always free.
