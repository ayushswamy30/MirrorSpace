Privacy Policy

Lowkei · Effective 10 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-10

The short version

  • What you write in Lowkei — check-ins, vent pages, Mirror chats, your plan, letters, notes — is kept on your phone, encrypted. It is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Your writing leaves the phone only when you have switched on AI reflections and ask the Mirror something or ask for a reflection on a page. It passes through our server to an AI service to get an answer, and neither we nor that service keep it.
  • Our server holds a small account record: an anonymous ID, your email if you add one, your consent choices, and — if you use Circle — what you chose to share with your circle.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • You can download everything, or erase everything, from the You tab at any time.

This policy explains what personal data Lowkei (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles, why, where it goes, and the rights you have over it. 1. What stays on your phone

Most of Lowkei works without a server. The following is stored only on your device, in a database encrypted with SQLCipher. The key is held in your phone’s secure storage and never leaves it:

  • Check-ins: the feelings you choose and your energy for the day.
  • Vent pages, and any reflections written on them.
  • Your Mirror conversation, and the items on “What the Mirror knows”.
  • Sleep and wake times, whether entered by hand or read from Health Connect.
  • Steps, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability, if you allow Lowkei to read them.
  • Your safety plan, letters, experiments, and the record of times you opened crisis help.
  • Your answer to the age question, and app preferences such as appearance and reminders.

Your daily reading, patterns, Mind Chart, forecast and Wrapped are all calculated on the phone from this data.

Because this data is on your device, we cannot see it, recover it, or move it to a new phone for you. If you uninstall the app or lose the phone, it is gone. Use “Download everything” to keep a copy.

Circle — only if you use it

If you start or join a circle, and only after you agree to it on its own consent screen, our server also stores:

  • The name and picture you show your circle, and your invite code.
  • Who is in your circle, and the nudges you send and receive.
  • Today’s Inner Weather — one word, such as “fog” or “clear”.
  • That you are “running low”, when you say so. It clears itself after a day.
  • Which days of the week tend to be lighter or heavier for you, as seven numbers.

The people in your circle can see these. They never see your words, notes, pages, Mirror conversation, or which feelings you chose.

3. AI reflections

AI reflections are off unless you switch them on, during onboarding or later in You → Privacy. When they are on:

  • When you send a message to the Mirror, the conversation so far is sent to get a reply.
  • When you ask for a reflection on a vent page, the text of that page is sent.
  • If you also switch on “What the Mirror knows”, the patterns and notes on that page are sent with each Mirror message.

This text travels encrypted to our server, which passes it to Groq, the AI service that writes the reply, and returns the answer to your phone. Our server does not store the text or the reply. Groq does not use it to train models, and under our account settings does not retain it. The reply is saved only on your phone.

The Mirror is software. It is not a person, a therapist, or a medical service, and its replies can be wrong.

If you turn AI reflections off, nothing you write is sent anywhere; Vent keeps working and Mirror chat closes.

4. Voice

When you tap speak in the Mirror, your phone turns your speech into text using its own on-device speech recognition. Lowkei does not record audio, and no audio is sent to us or to anyone else. If you then send the text to the Mirror, it is handled as in section 3. Answers read aloud are spoken by your phone’s own text-to-speech.

5. Health Connect

On Android, Lowkei can read from Health Connect, but only with your permission, which you can withdraw in Health Connect at any time:

  • Sleep — to show your nights and include them in your patterns.
  • Steps, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability — asked for separately, as a second step, to show how your body and your days move together.

This data stays on your phone. It is not sent to our server, to the AI service, or to anyone else. Lowkei never writes to Health Connect and reads nothing else from it.

Lowkei’s use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not use it for advertising, do not sell it, and do not use it to decide whether you are eligible for credit, insurance or anything else.

6. Notifications

The daily reminder is scheduled on your phone; no server is involved. Circle alerts are sent from our server through Expo’s push service and Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging. They contain no names, feelings or weather — only a line such as “Someone in your circle is thinking of you.”

8. How long we keep it

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it, erase everything, or uninstall the app.
  • Your account record and Circle data stay until you erase your account. “Running low” clears itself after a day.
  • Text sent for AI reflections is not kept by our server or by Groq.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, and wherever you live, you can:

  • Access your data — You → Your data → Download everything gives you one file with what is on your phone and what our server holds.
  • Withdraw consent — switch off readings or AI reflections in You → Privacy, leave or stop sharing with your circle, or remove Health Connect access. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent, and it doesn’t affect anything that happened before.
  • Correct your data — edit or delete entries in the app, change your circle name and picture, or write to us.
  • Erase your data — You → Erase → erase everything deletes your account on our server and everything on the phone. It cannot be undone.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to — write to us.
  • Raise a grievance — write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We will reply within 30 days at most. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Age

Lowkei is for people aged 18 and over. The app asks your age at the start and does not let anyone who says they are under 18 continue. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, write to us and we will erase it.

11. Security

Your writing is encrypted on the phone, and you can add an app lock. Everything between the app and our server travels over HTTPS. On the server, each account can reach only its own rows. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

12. Changes

If we change what we collect or where it goes, we will update this page and its version date. If a change affects something you consented to, the app will ask you again before it applies.
