# Lowkei brand kit

## In one line

A private journal and mood tracker that feels like a soft, dreamy place —
somewhere to notice how you are, lowkey.

## Name

**Lowkei** — always with a capital L in sentences. In the interface the
wordmark is lowercase, `lowkei`, followed by the small weather dot.

## The mark

A glassy, iridescent orb with a warm glowing centre — the Mirror's eye,
softened into light — floating in an airbrushed aura of periwinkle, pink,
peach and mint, with one four-point sparkle catching it.

- Source: `mobile/scripts/icon/icon.html`; render every size with
  `node mobile/scripts/icon/render.js` (app icon, Android adaptive layers and
  monochrome, notification icon, splash, site icons).
- Favicon: `site/public/favicon.svg`.
- Keep the orb centred with clear space of at least a quarter of its width.
  Don't recolour it flat, add text to it, or put it on a busy picture.

## Type

- **Fraunces** — headlines and anything Lowkei says to you.
- **DM Sans** — the interface: body, labels, buttons. Sentence case.
- **DM Mono** — numbers.

All three are SIL Open Font License.

## Pictures

Dreamy, airbrushed, softly grainy, glowing — pastel skies, soft hills,
flowers in focus and out, glassy jelly creatures, a little cosmic. The chosen
set is in `design/images/` (26 slots); `design/image-prompts.md` describes
each slot and the style line for making new ones. Text never sits directly on
a picture.

## Voice

Quiet, warm and plain. Short sentences. Say what something does and what it
won't do ("Your words stay on this phone"). Never diagnose, never score,
never hurry. The Mirror always says it's software, not a person or a
therapist. Crisis help is always one tap away and always free.

Do: "Where are you, right now?" · "A gentle nudge, once a day?"
Don't: "Unlock your best self!" · "You're 72% anxious."
