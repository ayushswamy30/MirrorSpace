# Lowkei — image prompts

26 original images for the app and the site. Generate each one with the
**style line** below in front of its prompt, save it with the **file name**
given, and put them all in `design/images/`. Then run
`python mobile/scripts/pictures.py` — it makes the app's copies — and they
replace the placeholders everywhere.

PNG or JPG, at the size given (or larger, same shape).

## Style line (put this in front of every prompt)

> Original illustration. Dreamy, airbrushed digital painting with a soft
> film-grain texture and a gentle glow. Luminous pastel palette — periwinkle
> blue, bubblegum pink, peach, lilac and mint — with soft-focus edges, light
> blooming around bright areas, and a few small four-pointed sparkles. Calm,
> tender, a little magical. No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no
> signature, no border.
